# Code of conduct

Be respectful and specific. Discuss the work, explain disagreements, and
give people room to correct mistakes. Welcome newcomers and respect
differences in background, experience, identity, and language.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention,
publication of private information, and repeated disruption are not
acceptable. These rules apply to project issues, reviews, discussions,
events, and communication made on the project's behalf.

Once the repository is public, report concerns privately to the maintainer
through the contact listed in the repository's community settings. That
contact must be configured before public launch. During this local setup,
use the existing private conversation with the maintainer.

The maintainer reviews reports, protects the reporter's privacy, and may
remove content, request a correction, restrict participation, or remove
access. Responses depend on severity and repeated behavior. Someone
involved in a complaint should not decide their own case; appoint an
uninvolved reviewer. Appeals go to that reviewer with the relevant facts.
