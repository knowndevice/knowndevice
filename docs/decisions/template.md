# RFC: concise decision title

Status: proposed. Owner: issue assignee. Related issue: add its reference.

Describe the problem and the behavior users need. Identify the affected
trust boundaries and public contracts.

Record two or three credible alternatives and their tradeoffs. State the
chosen approach, why it fits, and how to reverse or migrate it.

List the observable acceptance criteria, failure cases, and exact checks.
Record the review decision and unresolved questions before implementation.
